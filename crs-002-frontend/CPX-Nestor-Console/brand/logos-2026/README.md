# CPX 2026 logo - official mark (extracted from the brand kit)

Source: `Colors-CMYK.pdf` (CPX 2026 brand kit) pages 1 to 2. The 2026 mark is a NEW design
(not a recolour of the old "CP + arrow" logo): lowercase **cpx** with the **x** as a
double-chevron, the **left chevron in CPX Green** `#4CEE76`, the rest in Near Black `#040405`.

Vectors normalised to the official RGB brand hexes (the PDF is CMYK; pdftocairo converted
the green to ~#5CC46B and dark to ~#1F1F24 - these files use the exact #4CEE76 / #040405).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `cpx-logo-primary.svg` / `.png` | Dark mark on light backgrounds (transparent) |
| `cpx-logo-reverse.svg` / `.png` | White mark + green chevron on dark backgrounds (transparent) |
| `cpx-logo-on-purple.svg` / `.png` | White + green on the Dark Purple `#1E1847` brand background |

PNGs are ~2097×939 (600 dpi crop). SVGs are scalable (viewBox tight to the mark).
The older `../svg/` and `../png/` logos are the PRE-2026 (yellow CP) mark - retained for
history only; do not use on the new brand.
